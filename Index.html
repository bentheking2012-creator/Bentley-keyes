The Story

Bentley Carter Keyes was born on March 14th, 2012. His early years were not always easy, and the beginning of middle school was a particularly difficult period for him. Adjusting to a new stage of life brought challenges, but Bentley continued moving forward. As time went on, things began to change for the better, especially when he met people who would become some of his closest friends. Colton, Campbell, Beau, Donovan, and Anthony became important parts of Bentley's life and helped make his school years much better.

Bentley is the kind of person who is naturally curious. He likes learning about things, exploring ideas, playing games, and getting interested in whatever catches his attention. He also has a temper and can become frustrated, but beneath that strong personality is someone who cares deeply about the people around him. His family means a great deal to him, and he values the people who have been there for him throughout his life.
